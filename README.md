### Projects

- [**SABER-Math**](https://sabermath.insait.ai/)\
  An automated reranking benchmark for mathematical information retrieval. Developed ontology-based candidate retrieval using LLM topic assignment and tree-based similarity scoring.\
  `EMNLP 2026 — Main Conference` `ICML 2026 — AI for Math`
- **Competing Forms of Specialization in Mixture-of-Experts Models**\
  Studying how routers and experts share or exchange roles in learning group structure and predictive features. Derived high-dimensional learning dynamics and replica-symmetric equations, and compared theoretical predictions with SGD experiments.\
  `EPFL · Bachelor's thesis · Paper in preparation`
- [**Conditional Generative Drifting for Super-Resolution**](https://github.com/intsystems/2026-Project-208)\
  Extended generative drifting to conditional, one-step image super-resolution with multiple plausible outputs. Compared feature spaces for reconstruction diversity, fidelity, and low-resolution consistency.
- [**Physics-Informed Generative Modelling of Heavy-Ion Collisions**](https://github.com/ibragimovakr/PhysicsInformedSurrogate)\
  Developed a conditional flow-matching model combining complementary collision simulators. Designed physics-aware losses and separate discriminators for different particle groups.\
  `CHEP 2026 — Poster · Paper in preparation`
- [**Diffractive Deep Neural Networks (D2NN)**](https://github.com/ibragimovakr/D2NN_final)\
  Differentiable optical propagation with trainable phase masks. Developed physics-informed initialization using the Gerchberg–Saxton algorithm and wavefront matching, and studied robustness to phase defects and wavelength perturbations.
  `Best Talk — 68th Scientific Conference`
- [**Spin Glasses & Neural Networks**](https://github.com/ibragimovakr/spinglass)\
  Studied neural-network optimization through spin-glass models, TAP equations, and Monte Carlo annealing. Investigated connections between phase behavior, energy landscapes, and learning dynamics.
  `Best Talk — 67th Scientific Conference`
